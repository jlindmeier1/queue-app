Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email shasks-support@gmail.com and you'll get a reply from the person who makes Queue.

My lists didn't come back on a new phone

Lists are backed up to iCloud. Check that you're signed in to iCloud in the Settings app, with iCloud Drive turned on, then open Queue and give it a minute. Queue's Settings screen shows whether iCloud Backup is on.

Why doesn't a title have a Rotten Tomatoes score?

Scores come from Wikidata, where volunteers record them. Most well-known movies have one, but new releases and TV shows often don't yet. "See on Rotten Tomatoes" on the title's page always takes you there.

Where to Watch looks wrong

Streaming availability comes from JustWatch and follows your region in Settings. Services change their catalogs often, so it can lag by a day or two.
