Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Queue does not collect, store or sell any information about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your lists and ratings

Your lists, ratings, swipes and settings are stored on your iPhone. If you're signed in to iCloud, they're also saved to your own iCloud account so they come back on a new phone. That copy is held by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. We can't see it.

Looking up movies and shows

To show posters, details, scores and where to watch, the app asks these services about the titles you browse:

  • TMDB (movie and show details, and streaming availability from JustWatch)
  • Wikidata (Rotten Tomatoes scores)
  • YouTube (details of videos you save)

These requests say which title or video to look up. They don't include your name, your lists or anything else about you, though like any website these services can see your device's IP address. Links such as "See on Rotten Tomatoes" open those sites under their own privacy policies.

Tips

Tips are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Children

Queue collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
